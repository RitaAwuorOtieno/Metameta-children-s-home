What to Expect

A Safe and Rewarding Experience

We match volunteers thoughtfully so every contribution benefits both you and the children.

Requirements

  • Be at least 18 years old
  • Complete the application and orientation
  • Provide a valid ID and two references
  • Pass safeguarding checks where required
  • Commit to our child-protection guidelines

Benefits

  • Make a direct, lasting difference
  • Build skills and experience
  • Join a caring community
  • Receive guidance from our team
  • Get a volunteer recognition letter

Before You Begin

  • Choose an opportunity that suits you
  • Tell us your availability
  • Attend a short orientation
  • Agree your role with our coordinator
  • Start creating brighter days
